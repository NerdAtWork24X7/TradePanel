# Exchange marks

`binance.svg`, `bybit.svg`, `okx.svg`, `coinbase.svg`, `kraken.svg`,
`kucoin.svg`, `gateio.svg` and `bitget.svg` come from
[Aero25x/cryptocurrencies-exchanges-and-dexes](https://github.com/Aero25x/cryptocurrencies-exchanges-and-dexes),
licensed under the MIT License. The marks remain the trademarks of their
owners and are used only to identify the venue a symbol trades on.

`hyperliquid.svg` is a local monogram, not an official Hyperliquid asset.

`nse.svg` is a vector redraw of the NSE rosette, using the colours in the official
[NSE logo](https://nsearchives.nseindia.in/web/sites/default/files/2019-07/NSE%404x-100.jpg)
published on NSE's media-resources page. The mark remains a trademark of the
National Stock Exchange of India and is used only to identify the exchange.
